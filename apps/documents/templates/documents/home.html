Every document read by a second person

A register for the documents your organisation relies on. Somebody submits a document, a colleague approves or rejects it, and nothing waits unnoticed.

How it works

  1. 1. Submit

    Give a document a title, a text and tags. It waits for a review from that moment.

  2. 2. A colleague reviews

    Whoever submitted a document cannot approve it. Nobody signs off on their own work.

  3. 3. Nothing is forgotten

    A document that has waited more than 30 days is marked as overdue on the dashboard.
